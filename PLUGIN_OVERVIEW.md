Run many threads without losing the long-lived ones. Armada replaces the
sidebar thread list with captains on top, every thread in one list you
can filter by project, and quiet shelves for what you have settled or snoozed.

## What you get

- **Captains.** A captain is a long-lived thread for a project, with a
  character avatar of its own at the top of the sidebar. A project can have
  several, say one per ongoing task, each with its own memory folder.
- **Threads, each once.** Every thread is a card with its project, status,
  branch, pull request and harness. A thread a captain started shows that
  captain's face and name before its project, and the captain's row says how
  many of its threads are working, need you or failed. Sub-threads fold into their parent, which takes on
  the most urgent status among them. Pick a project to see only its threads.
- **One click to start.** The **+** next to Captains asks for a name (the
  project's, unless you type one), a project and an avatar (shuffle until one fits), then starts a captain thread that sets up
  its own notes file. Optional **Instructions** tell the captain how to work;
  they are kept in its `captain.md` and it follows them every turn.
- **Settle and snooze.** Hover a finished card for **Settle** and **Snooze until
  tomorrow**, or right-click for the snooze presets. Settled and snoozed
  threads fold into one-line shelves below the list. A snooze wakes on time,
  and any thread that starts working or needs you comes straight back,
  whatever you filed it as.

## Make a captain from any thread

Right-click a thread and choose **Make captain…**. A captain's menu has
**Edit captain…** (rename it, change its avatar, or change its instructions) and **Remove from
captains**. Removing only forgets the
captain; the thread stays. A shelved thread's menu has **Move to active**.

## Good to know

Rows behave like bb's own: click to open, cmd-click to open in a split, the
jump-to-thread shortcuts, bb's thread menu, and rename in place. Captains are
stored by the plugin on your bb server, so every device sees the same ones.
